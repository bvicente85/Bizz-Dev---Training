# Course: Time Management & Prioritisation
**Area:** Personal Effectiveness & Soft Skills | **Duration:** 7 hours | **Methodology:** A highly interactive and practical training, combining self-diagnosis, group discussion and priority-mapping exercises.

## Description and Objectives
Optimise work organisation, set priorities and enhance personal effectiveness.

* Recognise personal behavioural factors that affect time management.
* Apply priority management techniques (Pareto Principle, urgency vs. importance).
* Map and delegate tasks effectively to optimise time investment.
* Implement planning frameworks for daily, weekly and monthly organisation.
* Manage communication and boundaries to reduce work-related stress.

## Skills to Develop
* Differentiate between urgent and important tasks using priority management matrices.
* Identify and eliminate 'time parasites' (distractions, procrastination).
* Create realistic daily, weekly and monthly plans aligned with key role objectives.

## Detailed Syllabus

### SECTION 1: DISPELLING MYTHS & TIME PARASITES
* Time management principles and common productivity myths.
* Identifying 'Time Parasites': distractions, interruptions and procrastination.
* Self-Diagnosis: identifying personal time management styles and time-wasting patterns.

### SECTION 2: PRIORITY MANAGEMENT & TASK CONVERSION
* Setting priorities: operationalising importance vs. urgency criteria (Eisenhower Matrix).
* Converting role objectives into tasks: applying the Pareto Principle (80/20 rule).
* Delegation strategies: defining tasks, criteria and conditions for delegation.

### SECTION 3: EFFECTIVE PLANNING & WORK ORGANISATION
* Planning routines: structuring the day, week, month and year.
* Optimising low-yield periods and managing personal energy/workflow.
* Boundaries and assertiveness: managing interruptions and saying 'no' productively.

### Practical Exercise
* Conducting a personal time audit, mapping current tasks on the Eisenhower Matrix and drafting a weekly planning template.
