# Course: Product AI
**Area:** Artificial Intelligence / Product Management | **Duration:** 7 hours | **Methodology:** A highly interactive and practical training, combining product management methodologies (Scrum, Lean Product) with generative AI tools.

## Description and Objectives
Accelerate product discovery, backlog management and roadmapping using Artificial Intelligence.

* Apply AI tools to accelerate the product discovery phase and market mapping.
* Create and refine user personas and empathy maps using generative AI.
* Write high-quality user stories and Gherkin acceptance criteria with AI assistance.
* Use AI to categorise, prioritise and manage product backlogs (RICE, Kano model).
* Analyse quantitative and qualitative user data to support roadmap decisions.

## Skills to Develop
* Use AI for fast-paced market research, competitor analysis and user persona creation.
* Automate and optimise user story writing, acceptance criteria and backlog refinement.
* Leverage AI to analyse product metrics, customer feedback and define roadmaps.

## Detailed Syllabus

### SECTION 1: AI-POWERED PRODUCT DISCOVERY AND USER RESEARCH
* Market research and competitor analysis using advanced search and prompting.
* Generating data-driven user personas and user journey maps with LLMs.
* Identifying customer pain points and opportunities from raw review datasets.

### SECTION 2: BACKLOG REFINEMENT AND USER STORY WRITING
* Prompting strategies to write clear, structured User Stories.
* Generating comprehensive acceptance criteria (Given-When-Then / Gherkin syntax).
* AI-assisted estimation and story mapping techniques.

### SECTION 3: PRIORITISATION, ROADMAPPING AND PRODUCT ANALYTICS
* Applying prioritisation frameworks (Kano, RICE, WSJF) using AI models.
* Creating and visualising product roadmaps and release plans with generative tools.
* Analysing qualitative user feedback and NPS comments to generate product insights.

### Practical Exercise
* Conducting a mock product discovery, writing a feature backlog with user stories and creating a roadmap using Claude/ChatGPT.

**Facilitator:** Agile Product Consultant and AI Advocate with extensive experience leading product teams in tech companies.
**Sales Pitch:** The best training to help Product Managers double their discovery speed and maintain an immaculate backlog using AI.
