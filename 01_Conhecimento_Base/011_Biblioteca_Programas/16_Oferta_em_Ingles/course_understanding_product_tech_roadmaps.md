# Course: Understanding Product and Tech Roadmaps
**Area:** Personal Effectiveness & Soft Skills / Tech | **Duration:** 7 hours | **Methodology:** A practical and collaborative training, utilising Agile planning frameworks, story mapping simulations and roadmap drafting tools.

## Description and Objectives
Align business strategy with technology execution through collaborative roadmap planning.

* Translate the company's strategic vision into a concrete product backlog.
* Understand release planning concepts (Story Mapping, Cone of Uncertainty).
* Manage and prioritise backlogs, balancing new features and technical debt.
* Structure roadmaps that align business value with technical feasibility.
* Establish clear tracking metrics (Velocity, Burn charts) and communication channels.

## Skills to Develop
* Understand the roles of Product Owners and Engineering in roadmap creation.
* Plan releases using Story Mapping, Agile estimation and prioritisation frameworks.
* Draft and communicate flexible, value-driven product and technology roadmaps.

## Detailed Syllabus

### SECTION 1: PRODUCT VISION & RELEASE PLANNING
* Defining the product vision and aligning it with business objectives.
* Release planning fundamentals: managing expectations under the Cone of Uncertainty.
* Story Mapping: visualising the user journey and defining MVP (Minimum Viable Product) releases.

### SECTION 2: BACKLOG MANAGEMENT & PRIORITISATION
* Writing effective User Stories and managing the product backlog.
* Balancing business value, user needs and technical debt.
* Backlog refinement: preparing items for development sprints.

### SECTION 3: BUILDING & COMMUNICATING ROADMAPS
* Structuring flexible, goal-oriented roadmaps (Outcome-based vs. Feature-based).
* Aligning product roadmaps with technical and architectural dependencies.
* Sprint reviews, progress reporting and communicating roadmap changes to stakeholders.

### Practical Exercise
* Conducting a Story Mapping session for a new product feature, estimating development effort and drafting a 3-month product and tech roadmap.

## Trainer Profile / Sales Notes
**Facilitator:** Scrum Alliance Certified Product Owner and Agile Coach with a background in engineering leadership.
**Sales Pitch:** Equips non-tech and product roles with the framework to align priorities smoothly with development teams.
