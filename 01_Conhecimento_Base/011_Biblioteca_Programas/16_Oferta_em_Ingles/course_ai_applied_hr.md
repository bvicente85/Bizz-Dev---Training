# Course: AI Applied to HR
**Area:** Artificial Intelligence / Human Resources | **Duration:** 7 hours | **Methodology:** Practical and active training, focused on hands-on exploration of Generative AI applications for Human Resources.

## Description and Objectives
Integrate Artificial Intelligence into your HR processes to improve efficiency, talent acquisition and organisational workflows.

* Learn to use LLMs effectively for writing job descriptions and onboarding guides.
* Use AI to automate repetitive administrative tasks and feedback collation.
* Understand the compliance and security requirements under the EU AI Act.

## Skills to Develop
* Understand how AI can support the entire employee lifecycle.
* Explore the role of HR in mobilising digital literacy and an AI mindset.
* Discover practical possibilities for using AI in daily HR routines.

## Detailed Syllabus

### SECTION 1: THE STRATEGIC ROLE OF HR IN THE DIGITAL TRANSITION WITH AI
* Digital literacy as a key competency for HR.
* Leading the AI mindset in the organisation: from recruitment to offboarding.
* How to narrate this transition clearly and inspiringly.

### SECTION 2: AI AT THE SERVICE OF ALL HR AREAS
* Recruitment and payroll optimisation (brief overview).
* Training and development: building adaptive learning plans with AI.
* Offboarding: collecting feedback and mobility planning.

### SECTION 3: AI TOOLS IN DAILY ROUTINES - WHAT IS POSSIBLE TODAY
* How to transform documents into information maps: structured extraction from reports.
* Effective prompting for HR tasks: advanced templates and custom instructions.
* Data interpretation with AI for decision support (without technical dashboards).

### Practical Exercise
* Designing an AI-based onboarding plan and feedback questionnaire using LLM prompts.

## Trainer Profile / Sales Notes
**Facilitator:** Senior HR and AI Consultant with deep practical experience in modernising HR departments using generative technologies.
**Sales Pitch:** The ideal training for HR teams that want to transition from traditional operations to tech-enabled strategic talent management.
