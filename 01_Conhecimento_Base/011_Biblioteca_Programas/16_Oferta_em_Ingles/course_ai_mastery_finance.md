# Course: AI Mastery in Finance
**Area:** Artificial Intelligence / Finance & Accounting | **Duration:** 7 hours | **Methodology:** Hands-on practical training combining financial analysis frameworks with advanced AI tools.

## Description and Objectives
Master the application of Artificial Intelligence in financial planning, risk analysis, accounting automation and trading workflows.

* Apply AI techniques to clean, parse and structure complex financial datasets.
* Leverage LLMs to write financial code, formulas (Excel/DAX) and SQL scripts.
* Understand compliance, data security and privacy challenges in financial AI.

## Skills to Develop
* Automate data entry, invoice processing and basic accounting workflows with AI.
* Use AI models for financial forecasting, risk assessment and trend analysis.
* Implement AI assistants to generate narrative financial reports and dashboards.

## Detailed Syllabus

### SECTION 1: AI IN FINANCE & ACCOUNTING: AN OVERVIEW
* Impact of Generative AI on financial operations and decision-making.
* Automated accounting: invoice extraction, reconciliation and categorisation.
* Security protocols: hosting local LLMs vs. public cloud services for financial data.

### SECTION 2: PREDICTIVE FINANCE AND RISKS
* Financial forecasting: using AI models for revenue and cash flow projections.
* Risk assessment: detecting fraudulent transactions and anomalies with ML.
* Trading and risk analysis: market sentiment analysis and trading data interpretation.

### SECTION 3: FINANCIAL REPORTING AND DATA ANALYSIS
* Automated dashboard scripting (DAX, Excel formulas, SQL query building).
* Turning numbers into narratives: generating executive reports and investor updates.
* Evaluating model reliability: double-checking AI-generated financial outputs.

### Practical Exercise
* Creating an automated cash-flow forecasting prompt structure and generating a narrative report from a raw financial spreadsheet.

## Trainer Profile / Sales Notes
**Facilitator:** Financial Analyst and AI Developer with experience in implementing automated financial systems and risk modelling.
**Sales Pitch:** Bridges the gap between financial intelligence and machine learning, ensuring financial teams remain at the cutting edge.
