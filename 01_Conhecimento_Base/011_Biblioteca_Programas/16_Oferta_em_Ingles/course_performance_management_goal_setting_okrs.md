# Course: Performance Management & Goal Setting (OKRs)
**Area:** Personal Effectiveness & Soft Skills / HR | **Duration:** 7 hours | **Methodology:** An interactive training combining performance review concepts with the OKR framework, using practical exercises.

## Description and Objectives
Establish clear objectives, track key results and align performance across teams.

* Understand the link between organisational strategy and individual performance.
* Master the difference between KPIs (key performance indicators) and OKRs.
* Learn to write clear, action-oriented Objectives and measurable Key Results.
* Implement a continuous feedback culture to support goal achievement.
* Establish effective alignment meetings and tracking routines.

## Skills to Develop
* Understand the continuous performance management cycle (planning, monitoring and evaluation).
* Draft and structure impactful OKRs (Objectives and Key Results) for teams and individuals.
* Conduct constructive feedback loops and goal-alignment check-ins.

## Detailed Syllabus

### SECTION 1: PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT AND ALIGNMENT FOUNDATIONS
* The performance management cycle: planning, monitoring, evaluation and feedback.
* Aligning individual purpose with organisational goals and company culture.
* The role of managers and employees in continuous professional development.

### SECTION 2: THE OKR FRAMEWORK: FROM STRATEGY TO EXECUTION
* Introduction to OKRs: history, benefits and core principles.
* Differentiating KPIs (what to measure) from OKRs (what to achieve/change).
* Writing effective OKRs: defining qualitative Objectives and quantitative Key Results.

### SECTION 3: GOAL ALIGNMENT, FEEDBACK AND REVIEW ROUTINES
* Bidirectional alignment: horizontal (cross-functional) and vertical OKR mapping.
* Continuous tracking: weekly check-in routines and grading OKRs.
* Feedback loops and performance review interviews: best practices and active listening.

### Practical Exercise
* Designing and aligning an OKR set (Company -> Team -> Individual) and simulating a feedback review check-in.

**Sales Pitch:** Crucial for companies looking to align their teams around strategic goals and improve performance transparently.
