# Course: AI for Content Creation in Marketing
**Area:** Artificial Intelligence / Marketing | **Duration:** 7 hours | **Methodology:** A highly creative and practical hands-on training, combining copywriting frameworks with live generative AI tools.

## Description and Objectives
Produce high-quality copywriting, graphics and audio campaigns using Generative AI.

* Understand the fundamentals of Generative AI and its direct applications in marketing.
* Apply advanced copywriting formulas (AIDA, PAS) using prompt engineering.
* Design visual marketing materials and social media assets with text-to-image tools.
* Explore AI audio, video and synthetic voice generators for multimedia content.
* Manage copyright, intellectual property and data safety guidelines when using AI.

## Skills to Develop
* Master advanced prompt engineering for copywriting, messaging and campaign brainstorming.
* Generate professional marketing assets, images and graphics using AI generation tools.
* Optimise marketing workflows by automating content planning and scheduling.

## Detailed Syllabus

### SECTION 1: GENERATIVE AI IN MARKETING & COPYWRITING
* Introduction to Generative AI models (ChatGPT, Claude) for marketing workflows.
* Prompt engineering for copywriting: emails, blog posts, ad copies and SEO optimisation.
* AI-assisted brainstorming: campaign concepts, slogans and editorial calendars.

### SECTION 2: VISUAL & MULTIMEDIA CONTENT GENERATION
* Text-to-image tools: Midjourney, DALL-E and Canva AI for marketing graphics.
* Introduction to AI video generators and synthetic voiceovers.
* Maintaining brand consistency and visual guidelines in AI outputs.

### SECTION 3: WORKFLOW AUTOMATION & ETHICAL BEST PRACTICES
* Integrating AI into daily marketing workflows and social media scheduling.
* Intellectual property, copyrights and ethical use of AI-generated content.
* Data privacy and secure handling of brand and customer information.

### Practical Exercise
* Designing a complete digital marketing campaign: writing copy, generating visual assets and creating a social media calendar using AI.

## Trainer Profile / Sales Notes
**Facilitator:** Digital Marketer and Content Strategist specializing in AI content production and visual generation.
**Sales Pitch:** Allows marketing teams to 10x their content output while keeping creative quality and brand alignment intact.
