# Course: Reinventing the Customer Experience with AI
**Area:** Artificial Intelligence / Customer Service | **Duration:** 7 hours | **Methodology:** Practical live training with interactive labs, case studies and tool configurations.

## Description and Objectives
Leverage Generative AI to scale customer service operations, automate responses and improve customer satisfaction.

* Learn how to integrate AI tools in daily customer support operations.
* Write prompts that maintain a professional and consistent brand tone.
* Optimise resolution times and customer satisfaction metrics using AI.

## Skills to Develop
* Set up AI-powered customer service templates and workflows.
* Use AI to analyse customer sentiment and categorise support tickets.
* Implement conversational AI and chatbots to resolve common customer queries.

## Detailed Syllabus

### SECTION 1: AI IN CUSTOMER SERVICE: OPPORTUNITIES AND ETHICS
* The shift in customer support: AI as a co-pilot for service agents.
* Balancing automation and human touch: when to escalate.
* Data privacy and confidentiality when handling customer data.

### SECTION 2: PROMPT ENGINEERING FOR CLIENT SUPPORT
* Designing templates for email responses and chat templates.
* Sentiment analysis: identifying angry or frustrated clients and adapting responses.
* Translating and localising customer communications instantly with AI.

### SECTION 3: CONVERSATIONAL AI AND TICKET CATEGORISATION
* Introduction to conversational AI platforms and ticket automation.
* Classifying and routing support tickets automatically using LLM queries.
* Measuring and improving key metrics (CSAT, First Response Time) with AI.

### Practical Exercise
* Designing a detailed AI prompt matrix for handling complex customer complaints and setting up a basic support chatbot flow.

## Trainer Profile / Sales Notes
**Facilitator:** Customer Experience Specialist and AI Integrator with extensive background in customer support automation.
**Sales Pitch:** Essential for customer support teams aiming to scale their operation while maintaining high satisfaction rates and a human touch.
