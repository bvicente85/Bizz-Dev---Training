# Course: Communication & Influence
**Area:** Personal Effectiveness & Soft Skills | **Duration:** 7 hours | **Methodology:** A highly active and practical training, combining communication diagnostics, active listening role-plays and written workshops.

## Description and Objectives
Develop assertive communication, positive influence and effective collaboration.

* Understand the benefits of effective communication and the cost of communication breakdowns.
* Develop assertiveness and clarity when proposing ideas and driving decisions.
* Map stakeholders and adapt vocabulary and body language to the interlocutor's style.
* Structure written messages (emails, chat) objectively to avoid clutter and ambiguity.
* Build trust and positive influence within cross-functional teams.

## Skills to Develop
* Apply active listening and structured questioning to prevent misunderstandings and conflicts.
* Deliver assertive messages clearly in team meetings and collaborative sessions.
* Adapt communication styles to different stakeholders (peers, managers) to drive positive influence.

## Detailed Syllabus

### SECTION 1: TEAM COMMUNICATION ALIGNMENT & LISTENING
* The impact of clear communication vs. the operational consequences of poor communication.
* Message alignment: consistency in transmitting internal and external information.
* Active listening and questioning techniques to resolve conflicts and align expectations.

### SECTION 2: ASSERTIVENESS AND CLEAR DECISION-MAKING
* Rules and structural techniques for assertive communication.
* Promoting clarity and confidence when presenting ideas in collaborative contexts.
* Managing challenging conversations and balancing interests assertively to foster autonomy.

### SECTION 3: COMMUNICATION FLEXIBILITY & WRITTEN CLARITY
* Mapping stakeholders (peers, team members and leadership) and adapting communication styles.
* Structuring thoughts for written communication: instant messaging (Teams, Slack) vs. structured emails.
* Eliminating noise and redundancies in written texts to drive objective action.

### Practical Exercise
* Conducting a role-play simulation of a stakeholder alignment meeting and drafting a concise, action-oriented follow-up message.

## Trainer Profile / Sales Notes
**Facilitator:** Communications Specialist and Executive Coach focusing on corporate relations and interpersonal dynamics.
**Sales Pitch:** Crucial for teams that need to align fast, reduce internal noise and influence decisions assertively.
