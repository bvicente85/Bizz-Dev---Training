# Course: Public Speaking & Presenting with Impact
**Area:** Personal Effectiveness & Soft Skills | **Duration:** 14 hours | **Methodology:** A highly experiential and practical training. Participants will deliver multiple presentations and receive live feedback.

## Description and Objectives
Master the art of presentation, connect with your audience and deliver stories that stick.

* Identify personal strengths and areas of growth as a presenter.
* Align verbal, vocal and non-verbal communication (body language, voice tone, pacing).
* Apply storytelling techniques to make data and complex ideas memorable.
* Master techniques to control anxiety, stage fright and the 'fear of public speaking'.
* Engage, motivate and handle questions and objections from challenging audiences.

## Skills to Develop
* Control public speaking anxiety and build strong physical and vocal presence.
* Structure presentations using powerful storytelling frameworks (Hook-Point-Action).
* Design visual supports and slides that reinforce, rather than distract from, the message.

## Detailed Syllabus

### SECTION 1: THE PRESENTER'S PRESENCE & ANXIETY MANAGEMENT
* The psychology of public speaking: understanding and managing stage fright.
* Physical delivery: body posture, eye contact, hand gestures and spatial movement.
* Vocal delivery: controlling tone, volume, pitch and the power of pauses.

### SECTION 2: STRUCTURING THE MESSAGE & STORYTELLING
* Presentation structure: crafting compelling introductions and memorable conclusions.
* The power of Storytelling: character, conflict and resolution in business presentations.
* Making numbers speak: translating raw data into narratives that drive action.

### SECTION 3: AUDIENCE ENGAGEMENT & SLIDE DESIGN
* Designing high-impact visual supports (slide design, minimal text, imagery).
* Techniques to capture and sustain audience attention throughout the presentation.
* Managing Q&A sessions: handling difficult questions and audience objections with confidence.

### Practical Exercise
* Preparing and delivering a 5-minute presentation using storytelling, followed by live video review and individual feedback.
