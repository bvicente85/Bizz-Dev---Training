# Course: Collaboration & Cross-functional Alignment
**Area:** Personal Effectiveness & Soft Skills | **Duration:** 14 hours | **Methodology:** A highly interactive and practical training. Using simulation games, MIT's Theory U and Systems Thinking, participants will map processes.

## Description and Objectives
Break down silos, align internal stakeholders and streamline operational workflows.

* Establish clear quality standards for internal customer-supplier relationships.
* Mitigate interdepartmental friction by separating facts from subjective judgements.
* Master active listening levels (Theory U) to align cross-functional stakeholders.
* Analyse and map operational bottlenecks using internal customer journey mapping.
* Apply Systems Thinking to recognise how individual actions impact the organisation's ecosystem.

## Skills to Develop
* Apply MIT's Theory U (4 levels of listening) to improve interdepartmental communication.
* Map internal Customer Journeys to identify pain points and streamline processes.
* Develop a Systems Thinking mindset to understand team dependencies and feedback loops.

## Detailed Syllabus

### SECTION 1: THE FOUNDATIONS OF SYSTEMIC COLLABORATION
* Aligning individual purpose with organisational goals and team values.
* MIT's Theory U framework: opening the mind, heart and will for collective action.
* Establishing the internal customer-supplier mindset: serving and being served.

### SECTION 2: PROCESS FLUIDITY & INTERNAL JOURNEY MAPPING
* Mapping 'Pains' and 'Gains' between departments (Internal Customer Journey).
* Identifying and resolving the root causes of operational friction and delays.
* Overcoming bottlenecks and avoiding 'analysis paralysis' in joint projects.

### SECTION 3: SYSTEMS THINKING & ACTION PLANS
* The organisation as a living ecosystem: understanding feedback loops.
* How individual and team actions ripple across the cross-functional pipeline.
* Co-creating action plans and metrics to reinforce interdepartmental alignment.

### Practical Exercise
* Mapping a real cross-functional process between two departments, identifying friction points and drafting a collaborative Service Level Agreement (SLA).

## Trainer Profile / Sales Notes
**Facilitator:** Systems Architect and Facilitator trained in MIT Theory U and Agile management methodologies.
**Sales Pitch:** Highly effective for organisations suffering from silos, helping teams align dynamically and reduce delivery delays.
