# Course: Compliance and Governance of Artificial Intelligence (AI)
**Area:** Artificial Intelligence / Compliance | **Duration:** 7 hours | **Methodology:** An operational and practical training, combining legal and regulatory frameworks with compliance templates and auditing simulations.

## Description and Objectives
Transform AI Act and GDPR legal requirements into practical governance and risk management processes.

* Understand the obligations under the European AI Act and GDPR applied to AI systems.
* Implement the ISO/IEC 42001 (AI Management System) and NIST AI RMF guidelines.
* Identify and mitigate AI-specific risks, including biases, lack of transparency and data privacy issues.
* Establish human oversight and design the technical documentation required by law.
* Clarify the roles of DPOs, CISOs and Compliance Officers in the AI lifecycle.

## Skills to Develop
* Classify AI systems according to the risk categories of the EU AI Act.
* Implement ISO/IEC 42001 and NIST AI RMF frameworks within the organisation.
* Conduct AI risk assessments and document technical compliance requirements.

## Detailed Syllabus

### SECTION 1: AI GOVERNANCE CHALLENGES AND REGULATIONS
* Specific AI risks: bias, transparency, explainability and data sovereignty.
* EU AI Act: Risk classification (Unacceptable, High Risk, Limited, Minimal) and compliance obligations.
* AI and GDPR: Principles, Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA) and automated decisions (Article 22).

### SECTION 2: STANDARDS AND GOVERNANCE FRAMEWORKS
* ISO/IEC 42001: Operational management and organisational maturity for AI.
* NIST AI RMF: Mapping, measuring and governing AI risks.
* Complementarity between ISO 27001 (Security) and ISO 42001 (AI).

### SECTION 3: IMPLEMENTATION, AUDITING AND PORTFOLIO MANAGEMENT
* Compliance for internal Generative AI: chatbot deployment and vendor risk management.
* Portfolio management: risk-ranking and inventorying internal AI projects.
* Simplified audit checklists and setting up AI ethics committees.

### Practical Exercise
* Conducting a compliance audit simulation for an internal AI tool and drafting a risk mitigation plan.

## Trainer Profile / Sales Notes
**Facilitator:** Legal Consultant and CISO with deep expertise in DGERT and European compliance frameworks (ISO 42001/27001).
**Sales Pitch:** Crucial for compliance and IT leadership to ensure all company AI initiatives comply with the new EU AI Act without stopping innovation.
