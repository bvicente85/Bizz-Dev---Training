# Training Programme: Security & Risk Management and GDPR / Data Protection

## 1. Course Title
**Security & Risk Management and GDPR / Data Protection**

## 2. Subtitle
*Bespoke Intensive Training Programme for Cegid's IT IS Teams*

---

## 3. Background
The management of **Cegid** has made the decision to invest in developing the **Cybersecurity and Data Protection (GDPR)** skills of their teams.

Skolae Training has always offered in its learning solutions portfolio specific programmes for developing **risk management and compliance** skills that can enhance better **operational alignment and process security** within teams.

Good **security and compliance posture** in organisations has a direct impact on organisational climate, productivity, and consequently, on the business.

We contextualise that addressing **information security and GDPR** means integrating key technical topics such as **risk management in SaaS, GDPR Article 32 ↔ ISO 27001 / SOC 2 controls mapping, personal data breach management, and Shadow IT prevention**.

---

## 4. Purpose
To equip participants with the skills to actively manage information security risks and ensure operational compliance with the GDPR within Cegid's B2B ecosystem.

---

## 5. Target Audience
Members of Cegid's IT IS (Internal Information Systems) team (Portugal, Spain, and France), including security analysts, security engineers and architects, incident response teams, IT/Cloud operations staff, and security managers.

---

## 6. Competencies to Develop
*   Technological Risk Management in SaaS environments
*   GDPR/Data Protection Compliance in a B2B Context
*   Personal Data Breach Triage and Incident Management
*   Access Governance and HR/Payroll Data Protection
*   Shadow IT Prevention and Security Assessment

---

## 7. Specific Objectives
By the end of this training programme, participants will be able to:
*   Assess and mitigate security risks associated with personal data in SaaS and multi-tenant environments.
*   Relate and map GDPR Article 32 security requirements to ISO 27001 and SOC 2 control frameworks.
*   Conduct triage of security incidents and comply with the 72-hour rule for notifying personal data breaches to customers and authorities.
*   Evaluate the impact of new tool acquisitions (Shadow IT) from a security and privacy perspective.
*   Manage temporary access rights and protect highly sensitive databases (e.g., payroll data) in daily operations.

---

## 8. Methodology
The learning path is built on the **"Learning by Doing"** model and active training methodologies:
*   **Active and Interactive Approach:** Virtual synchronous sessions (via Microsoft Teams) focused on group discussions and resolving real-world daily cases brought by participants.
*   **Progressive Segmentation (Baseline):** An initial maturity assessment questionnaire will segment participants into appropriate learning paths (General level with a 3.5-hour foundation session and Advanced level with the full 7-hour session).
*   **Practical Workshops & Simulations:**
    *   *Workshop:* Defining a minimum security control set for a new cloud-based payroll module.
    *   *Tabletop Exercise:* A hands-on incident response simulation of compromised credentials in a multi-tenant environment.
*   **Multilingual Delivery:** Sessions delivered in English, Spanish, or French by native, senior instructors.

---

## 9. Detailed Programme

### Module I: Security & Risk Management (Common to All Teams)

#### 1. Risk Management Fundamentals
*   Security risk in a SaaS and multi-tenant context: threats, vulnerabilities, impact.
*   Risk assessment and treatment: identifying, evaluating, and prioritising risks to personal data.
*   The risk-based approach as the common language of GDPR, ISO 27001, and NIS2.
*   Roles and accountability: security, DPO, legal, and management.

#### 2. Security Controls and Frameworks
*   Technical and organisational measures: access control, encryption, pseudonymisation, segregation, backup, and resilience.
*   Security by design in the development lifecycle (SDLC).
*   Mapping controls across frameworks: GDPR Article 32 ↔ ISO 27001 ↔ SOC 2.
*   Customer assurance: audits, certifications, and evidence.

### Module II: GDPR / Data Protection & Incident Response (Advanced Group Only)

#### 3. GDPR Essentials for Security Professionals
*   Scope and key concepts: personal data, special categories, processing.
*   Personal data in technical environments: IPs, identifiers, logs, telemetry.
*   Data protection principles and lawful bases — legitimate interests as the basis for security operations.
*   Controller, processor, and sub-processor: where Cegid stands in each product line.

#### 4. Security of Processing and Data Protection by Design
*   Article 32: what “appropriate measures” means in practice.
*   Data protection by design and by default (Article 25).
*   Data Protection Impact Assessments (DPIAs): triggers and the security team's input.
*   *Practical Workshop:* Defining the minimum control set for a new cloud payroll module.

#### 5. Personal Data Breach Management
*   Security incident vs. personal data breach: definitions and triage.
*   The 72-hour rule and Cegid's duty as processor to notify customers without undue delay.
*   Notification to authorities and communication to data subjects.
*   Documentation, evidence, and the internal breach register.
*   *Tabletop Exercise:* Compromised credentials in a multi-tenant environment.

#### 6. Data Protection in Daily Security Operations
*   Logging, monitoring, and retention.
*   Production data in test and development environments.
*   International transfers and vetting new tools and vendors (Shadow IT).
*   Data subject rights meeting infrastructure: what deletion really means.
*   The regulatory landscape ahead: NIS2, DORA, and the EU AI Act (including AI embedded in Cegid products).

## 10. Case Study / Social Proof

### Case: BBVA (Financial Sector)
*   **The Challenge:** The need to recall and consolidate core data protection (GDPR) concepts for the team to apply consistently in daily tasks, mitigating compliance risks — a challenge identical to the one Cegid faces with its IT IS team.
*   **The Skolae Approach:** Delivery of an intensive 7-hour training programme fully customised to the participants' profiles and banking business reality, mapping legal obligations to practical daily scenarios ("what can and cannot be done").
*   **The Impact (ROI):** Full consistency in data protection compliance across branch networks and internal operations, successfully mitigating compliance risks and potential regulatory penalties.
