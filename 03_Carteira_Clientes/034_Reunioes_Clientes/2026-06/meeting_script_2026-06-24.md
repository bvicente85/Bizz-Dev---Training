# Meeting Script: GDPR & Risk Management Training (Cegid)
**Date:** 24 June 2026  
**Target Duration:** ~3-4 minutes (Opening & Pitch)  
**Tone:** Conversational, professional, natural British English (non-mechanical).

---

### Part 1: Welcome & Introductions (~1 min)
*Goal: Set a warm, professional tone and introduce the participants.*

**[Smile, look at the camera]**

"Hello everyone, lovely to see you all. Thank you so much for taking the time to join us today. 

Right then, since we’ve got quite a few people on the call and a lot of ground to cover, shall we get cracking?

Just by way of a quick introduction, I’m Bruno Vicente, and I look after the partnership with Cegid here in Portugal. Joining me today is Pedro Gonçalves, who is our lead expert and trainer in cybersecurity and data protection. 

I know Dalila is on the call, and I believe we also have some of the technical and area managers joining us from Spain and France as well. It’s a real pleasure to have you all with us."

---

### Part 2: The Skolae Pitch (~2 mins)
*Goal: Briefly introduce Skolae, our international setup, Iberian management, and bespoke approach.*

"Before we dive into the technical details, I thought it might be helpful to give you a very brief run-down of who we are at Skolae and how we operate, especially given the international scope of this project.

Broadly speaking, Skolae is an international corporate training group headquartered in France, with a strong footprint across Spain, Belgium, Luxembourg, and Portugal. 

Now, what makes this setup work particularly well for companies like Cegid is that we manage the entire Iberian operation—both Portugal and Spain—from right here in Lisbon. 

This means you get the best of both worlds: you have a single, local point of contact in me, but with the backing of legal entities and native trainers on the ground in both Spain and France. It makes things like local invoicing, compliance, and navigating Spain’s FUNDAE system completely hassle-free.

When it comes to the training itself, we cover a broad range of areas, but for teams like yours, our core focus tends to be on technology and security. We specialise in delivering bespoke programmes in **Cybersecurity and Risk Management**, **Data Governance**, **Cloud Engineering**, and **Agile methodologies**. We also work a lot on **leadership and feedback skills tailored for technical managers**, which often comes in handy in engineering environments.

We don't really do off-the-shelf, generic courses. Our focus is entirely on bespoke, highly practical programmes. We want to ensure that your IT and dev teams aren't just sitting through slides, but are actually getting their hands dirty with real-world scenarios that match their daily workflows."

---

### Part 3: Handing Over to the Client (~30 secs)
*Goal: Transition the floor to Dalila and the managers to explain their needs.*

"But enough from me. The main reason we’re here today is to listen. We want to get a clear picture of your current setup, the challenges you’re facing, and what you’re hoping to achieve with this training, specifically around GDPR, data protection, and security & risk management across the different regions.

So, Dalila, perhaps I could hand over the floor to you and the team to give us a bit of a brief on your expectations. Over to you."

---

### Part 4: Transition to the Trainer (Pedro)
*Goal: Bring the trainer in once the client has finished speaking.*

**[After the client finishes speaking, nod and validate]**

"That’s brilliant, thank you so much for that context. It’s incredibly clear.

To make sure we tailor the contents and the practical exercises precisely to what your teams do on a day-to-day basis, I’d like to bring in Pedro. 

Pedro, I’ll hand over to you to ask some of your diagnostic questions and run through the technical specifics with the managers. Please, fire away."

---

### Part 5: Concluding the Call & Next Steps (~1 min)
*Goal: Summarise the next steps, set expectations, and close the meeting professionally.*

**[Take back the floor once the technical discussion is finished]**

"Right then, if there are no further questions on the technical front, I think we have a very clear picture of what you need. 

Just to recap our next steps: Pedro and I will put our heads together to draft a bespoke proposal. We will map out the training structure for the three countries—Portugal, Spain, and France—along with the commercial details. I'll also make sure to factor in the logistics for local invoicing in Spain.

We’ll aim to get that over to you, Dalila, by early next week—shall we say Tuesday? How does that sound?

**[Wait for Dalila's response]**

Splendid. Thank you all once again for your time today. It’s been incredibly helpful. 

Have a lovely rest of the day, and we'll speak very soon. Bye now!"
